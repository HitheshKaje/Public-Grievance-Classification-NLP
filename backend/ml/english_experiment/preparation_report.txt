2. LEAKAGE-AWARE PARTITIONING METHODOLOGY (STEP 2):
--------------------------------------------------
  - Grouping Strategy: High-similarity Template Component Grouping.
  - Rationale: During synthetic generation of the 6 expansion departments, combinatorial
    slot filling produced 4,286 record pairs with token Jaccard similarity between 0.60 and 0.70.
    A naive random split would place highly similar template instances into both train and test,
    causing artificial metric inflation (test leakage).
  - Implementation: Built a similarity graph connecting all record pairs with Jaccard >= 0.60.
    Identified connected components and partitioned entire template components together.
  - Water Supply Handling: Because Water Supply pilot/batch records exhibit natural vocabulary diversity
    with 0 pairs >= 0.60, records were stratified evenly across all 7 problem types.
  - Result: Zero pairs with Jaccard >= 0.60 cross any partition boundary.
